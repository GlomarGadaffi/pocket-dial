Registration and Learn

Learn adopts an unknown phone on first contact and locks the extension to that device on its next registration, if no other phone claimed it first. Until then, or if the phone is on another subnet or shares a router's MAC with other phones, another phone can register on the same extension.

The adopted-phone roster, Forget and Forget all learned are in Devices.
