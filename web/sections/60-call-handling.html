Call handling

Ring and hunt groups

Ring all or hunt. Members are extension numbers, separated by commas. Outside lines are routed under Routing.

Empty members deletes the group.

Parked calls

Read-only. Park and pick-up are not controlled from this page.

Reserved extension ranges

Fixed in firmware and shown for reference. The firmware refuses these numbers for registration and in config, so they cannot be used as extensions, group members or forward targets. Not editable here.
